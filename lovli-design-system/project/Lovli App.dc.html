What the flow protects
  • Two taps to a reply. Language and vibe carry over from last time; platform and memory stay tucked behind one row.
  • The wait has a personality. Three phases of microcopy over a skeleton shaped like the card that's coming — never a spinner.
  • The read is words. One of three: Not into it · Mixed signals · Leaning interested. No gauge to screenshot and argue about.
  • Editing is the point. Every card says so, and the tap target is the text itself.
