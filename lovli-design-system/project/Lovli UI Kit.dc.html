16 The decode
‹ The decode
OVERALL VIBE
Leaning interested.
Not into itMixed signalsLeaning interested
POSITIVE SIGNS
✦ She asks follow-up questions — that's real curiosity, not politeness.
✦ She remembers small details you mentioned days ago.
WATCH-OUTS
✦ Plans stay vague from her side — she hasn't committed to anything yet.
WHAT'S REALLY GOING ON

She likes the attention and probably likes you — but she's comfortable in the texting stage and isn't going to move it forward herself.

YOUR NEXT MOVE

If I were your wingman: stop matching her vagueness. One clear, low-pressure plan — "chai Sunday, 5pm?" — and you'll know where you stand.

Likely outcome: she says yes, or she gives you the clarity you actually need.
